- **License**: Business Source License (BSL).
- **Vibe**: You are a developer who loves managing your own Linux boxes and Docker containers.

_Build your own SaaS on top of Dyrected._

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What counts as a site?**
A site is one Dyrected project — its own set of collections, globals, and admin users. Each client you build for typically gets one site.

**Can I switch plans?**
Yes, upgrade or downgrade anytime. If you upgrade mid-cycle you're only charged the difference.

**What happens if I exceed my storage?**
We'll notify you before anything breaks. You can upgrade your plan or purchase additional storage.

**What is the Business Source License?**
BSL means the source code is publicly available and free to self-host. The one restriction is that you cannot offer Dyrected itself as a hosted service to others — that's what our cloud plans are for. After 4 years, the code converts to Apache 2.0 automatically.

**Can I self-host for commercial client projects?**
Yes. Building client websites on a self-hosted Dyrected instance is completely free under BSL. You only need a paid plan if you want us to run the infrastructure for you.

**I'm building a SaaS and want to embed Dyrected. What do I need?**
The Multi-Tenancy License. [Get in touch →](#) and we'll walk you through it.

**Do you offer refunds?**
Yes. If you're not satisfied within the first 14 days, we'll refund you — no questions asked.
